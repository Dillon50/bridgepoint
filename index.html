The problem

Your customers are searching. Are you there?

About 47,000 businesses across Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach have no website. That number comes from applying Clutch's 2025 finding that 17 percent of U.S. small businesses have no site to the 275,000 small businesses the U.S. Census counts in those three counties.

Meanwhile 97 percent of consumers read online reviews of local businesses before choosing one (BrightLocal, 2026), and 75 percent judge whether a business is credible by its website (Stanford Web Credibility Research). When someone searches for a barber in Coral Springs or a plumber in Boca Raton and your business never appears, the call goes to the competitor who does.

I have sat across the counter from owners who book appointments in a paper notebook and watch walk-ins go to the shop down the street. The food, the cut, the work is better at their place. They lose because nobody can find them.
